The shorts

How each one was made

  1. The card is the cast. Moving pieces are cut out of the printed card and moved; the holes they leave are filled with the card's own colours. Nothing in the card is redrawn or generated.
  2. One operator per card. Each short follows one prompt from the SCION animation swarms: its theorem, its primitives and its numeric test. The test is computed in the page.
  3. Archive film, the CINEOSIS way. CINEOSIS (in the Bolas lab) set its figures into unaltered archive shots from the cineosis-lab library, which were found through movingimagearchive.com's shot search, one plain-language query per shot. These shorts use the same library the other way round: the archive comes into the card, as a mirror, a screen, a reflection, a sky, a fire, a backdrop. Each plate is a cut-free stretch of one shot, trimmed, toned and masked, never painted. Its title is on screen and in the ledger.
  4. Every frame from its time. Each short is one page (shorts/stage.js) that can draw any frame from the time alone. shorts/tools/render.mjs photographs every frame and adds a soundtrack synthesised from the page's cues. Nothing in the sound is sampled.
  5. A blind critic. A reviewer who had not seen the intent described contact sheets of all seven. Two failed and four partly read; each was fixed once and not reviewed again.
