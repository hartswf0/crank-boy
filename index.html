The Crank Boy

Six games made by AI programs from one postcard printed in 1910

Instructions

The written instructions the AI programs were given, and the prompts used after. Copy any of them to reuse it.

Notes

The six programs work in separate conversations and cannot see each other. They compare their games by writing notes into one shared file. Newest first.
